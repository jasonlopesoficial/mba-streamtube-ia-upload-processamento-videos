# GET /videos/{slug}/download and /thumbnail Test Plan

## Application Overview

Anonymous download of a `ready` video as an attachment named after the original file, and delivery of the JPEG thumbnail generated by the worker.

## Test Scenarios

### 1. Download and thumbnail

**Setup:** `beforeAll` bootstraps `AppModule` with `main.ts` globals; `beforeEach` cleans the auth/video tables, registers an owner, uploads a known byte sequence named `"meu vídeo.mp4"` through the presigned multipart flow, completes it, stores a JPEG at `thumbnails/{id}.jpg` and marks the video `ready` with `thumbnail_key` in the DB.

#### 1.1. download-returns-attachment-with-original-bytes

**Covers AC:** #1
**Source:** auto
**Last sync:** 2026-10-24T10:20:20Z

**Steps:**
  1. GET /videos/{slug}/download anonymously
    - expect: status 200
    - expect: `Content-Disposition` starts with `attachment;` and contains `filename*=UTF-8''meu%20v%C3%ADdeo.mp4`
    - expect: body equals the uploaded bytes
